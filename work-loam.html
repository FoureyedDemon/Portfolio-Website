Simplifying Money Transfers in a Banking App

A mobile banking money transfer module designed through user research to make sending money faster, clearer, and easier to complete. The project focused on understanding users’ transfer needs and translating those insights into a simple, intuitive experience.

Role
Product Owner & Lead designer
Timeline
4 months
Team
Solo, with the two Suss founders
Tools
Illustrator, InDesign, figma, Webflow

The problem

Users often found money transfers in mobile banking apps difficult to navigate. Unclear steps, unnecessary form fields, and limited feedback during the transfer process could make users unsure whether they were sending money correctly or whether the transaction had been completed.

This created a need for a simpler transfer experience that reduced confusion, improved confidence, and helped users complete transactions with less effort.

View more →

The approach

I began by conducting user research to understand how people currently send money through banking apps, the challenges they encounter, and what information they need to feel confident during a transfer.

Using these insights, I mapped the existing transfer journey and identified opportunities to simplify the experience. I created user flows to define the key steps, developed wireframes to explore the structure and interactions, and refined the solution into high-fidelity designs.

The final approach focused on making the transfer process clear and intuitive, from selecting a recipient and entering transaction details to reviewing and confirming the payment.

The outcome

The final design created a clearer and more intuitive money transfer experience for the mobile banking app. By simplifying the transfer flow and organizing the information into manageable steps, users could understand what was required and complete transactions with greater confidence.
