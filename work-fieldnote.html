Book Publishing Journey for Independent Authors

The book publishing app is designed for American authors who face challenges with writing and publishing. It simplifies the process by providing writing tools, customizable templates, voice input, text scanning, and collaboration features—all in one platform.

Role
Product Design & editorial design
Timeline
6 months
Team
1 designer, 3 engineers
Tools
Figma, type design, prototyping in code

The problem

81% of Americans want to write a book, but many struggle to turn their ideas into published books due to the increasing barriers to self-publishing. The process can be complex, expensive, and difficult to navigate, creating challenges for aspiring authors who want to publish independently.

View more →

The approach

I used the Design Thinking approach to understand the challenges aspiring authors face throughout the writing and self-publishing journey. I began by researching their experiences to identify the key barriers they encounter, from developing their ideas to formatting, editing, and preparing their books for publication.

Using these insights, I defined the core problems and explored potential solutions that could make self-publishing more accessible. I ideated around features such as customizable book templates, essential writing tools, voice input, text scanning, and collaboration, with the goal of bringing the entire writing process into one platform.

I then prototyped and tested the proposed experience with users, using their feedback to refine the flows and improve usability. This iterative process helped shape a simpler and more intuitive platform that supports authors from their first idea through to preparing their book for publication.

The outcome

The final product created a simpler, end-to-end experience for aspiring authors to write, prepare, and publish their books. By bringing essential writing and publishing tools into one platform, authors could move through the process without having to rely on multiple disconnected tools.

Features such as customizable templates, voice input, text scanning, and collaboration gave authors greater flexibility throughout the book creation process. I also introduced a feature that allows publishers to publish directly to Amazon Kindle, reducing the friction involved in taking a completed manuscript from the platform to a publishing channel.
